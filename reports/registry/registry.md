## Warnings attached to specific entries

- **`oracle.teacher_endpoint_scoring`** — Must never be used as an amortization denominator: selector and evaluator are the same model, and the population is the training split.
- **`oracle.teacher_best_of_4_heldout`** — THIS is the teacher oracle. It is NOT the +0.0485 noise-seed oracle.
- **`oracle.student_noise_best_of_4_heldout`** — A DIFFERENT intervention from the teacher oracle. Shallow OOF prediction of reward from raw noise is r = -0.0123, capping this route near +0.0015. This does not refute Noise Hypernetworks, which learns noise modulation end to end.
- **`result.C1_selected_trajectory_transfer`** — A null for THIS objective and setting; not a theorem that best-of-K cannot be amortized. Prompt idx in this directory is NOT comparable to the idx in exp0/primary_headroom: the two evaluations share zero prompts.
