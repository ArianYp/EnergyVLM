L(θ) = LCD(θ) − λ · r(θ),  λ = 15.5 (set so ‖∇r‖ ≈ 20% of ‖∇LCD‖ on the first 8 updates; the ratio drifts to 25–37% by the end)  → clip global norm to 1 → AdamW step on θ (student only)
∂LCD/∂θflows from the loss into x̂0 and into the student weights only. The teacher, its targets x0,k and the trajectory are detached. Nothing in branch B is touched by this gradient.
∂r/∂θflows backward through DINOv2 (frozen weights, input gradient only) → preprocessing → VAE decoder (frozen weights, input gradient only) → x̂0 → student weights. No weight of DINOv2 or the VAE changes; they act as a fixed differentiable measuring instrument. The gradient is nearly orthogonal to ∂LCD (cosine 0.04 at the probe points).
variantprojector-reward arms: ∂r̂ flows through P (frozen weights) into the student; in the refreshed arm a separate loss Lrefresh = [1 − ⟨P(x̂0), DINO(dec(x̂0))⟩] + replay(cosine + ranking KL) updates P only, with x̂0 detached from the student.
monitorevery 100 updates the 32 latest x̂0 are decoded to 8-bit images and scored by the offline PIL path; logged as the true score next to the reward, plus the reward-path score on the same latents (gap 0.000 ± 0.001 for the exact reward, −0.17 for the projector). No gradient.
